title: "October 1st, 2024: federal holiday"
date: 2024-09-25
summary: "On Tuesday, September 24, 2024, the Chamber of Deputies approved the draft decree by which Section VII of Article 74 of the Federal Labor Law will be amended to establish October 1st of every six years as a mandatory holiday on the occasion of the transmission of the Federal Executive Power, instead of December 1st."

The Decree must go through the legislative process until its publication in the Official Gazette of the Federation.

Notwithstanding that the Decree is not yet published in the Official Gazette of the Federation, given the imminent nature of its upcoming publication, and the systematic interpretation of the Federal Labor Law with the Political Constitution of the United Mexican States, it is recommended to grant employees October 1st, 2024 as a holiday, considering that employees who work on October 1st of this year will be entitled to receive a triple salary.

We will keep you informed about the corresponding updates of the legislative process and we remain at your service for any doubt, clarification, or comment that may arise with respect of the foregoing.

September 2024
