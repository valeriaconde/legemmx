As previously communicated to you over the years, Mexican commercial companies are required to comply with specific annual corporate legal obligations, including: (i) approval of the financial statements for the fiscal year ended; (ii) ratification or revocation of the Board of Directors or the Sole Manager (as applicable); and (iii) the ratification or revocation of the Statutory Auditor (if applicable). In addition to the foregoing, and as a result of several legal reforms that have occurred over the last 10 years, which were duly and timely communicated to you, Mexican commercial companies are also required to comply with a specific compliance program (the “Compliance Program”).

The Compliance Program that Legem Attorneys at Law, SC (“Legem”) provides to its clients seeks the creation and implementation of internal rules that guide certain activities, as well as the behavior of employees, taking as a reference the mandatory legal provisions in Mexico, while mitigating legal risks with third parties and government regulatory agencies.

The Compliance Program is developed based on a broad and diverse body of laws and regulations that are constantly updated; therefore, its implementation takes place in various stages in the short, medium, and long term, and may require modifications and adaptations based on the criteria established by the regulatory agencies or the resolutions issued by the corresponding courts.

The Compliance Program includes:

1. Integrity Policy (Anticorruption)
2. Controlling Beneficiary Identification
3. Psychosocial Risk Factors (NOM-035-STPS-2018)
4. Personal Data Privacy
5. Anti–Money Laundering (PLD)
6. Internal Conduct (Workplace Rules – Harassment Prevention Protocol)
7. Intellectual Property

As mentioned above, the Compliance Program involves several actions to be carried out by each area, which are shared with the client once the tasks have been completed; however, the Compliance Program must be reviewed on an annual basis, at the latest, to ensure that clients remain in good standing in this important area.

As part of the regular implementation of the Compliance Program, the client selects the actions it wishes to begin with, and in response, Legem defines the objective and scope of the services related to each action, as well as the respective quotation.

At present, the Mexican government is paying particular attention to compliance with these obligations; therefore, the sanctions that may result from non-compliance could be substantial.

Legem, as a Mexican legal business consultant strongly recommends that its clients contact us to discuss in detail the Compliance Program and each of the above-mentioned programs.
